# freestyle results after 2005

Hydra's two 2005 accounts scored 7.5/16 against the field; a Hydra-run account won PAL/CSS in 2006, followed by victories for the Rybka author's team.

The [Hydra team report](https://en.chessbase.com/post/hydra-mies-the-quarter-finals-of-freestyle-tournament) identifies Zor_champ as unassisted and Ares01 as human-assisted, with software problems on the latter: their respective scores were 3.5/8 and 4/8 after excluding the superseded Landa score.

Zor_champ won [edition two](https://en.chessbase.com/post/zor-champ-wins-freestyle-tournament); its 2006 report identifies Hydra's program and hardware, without establishing live human involvement that year. Rajlich's team won editions three and six, according to the [sixth-edition report](https://en.chessbase.com/post/rajlich-wins-sixth-pal-c-freestyle-tournament).

Nelson Hernandez's [December 2007 report](https://en.chessbase.com/post/che-festival-in-benidorm-where-a-new-genre-is-born) identifies Anson Williams as Ibermax's captain, winning edition seven with +6 =2 −1. Williams and helpers later won as Intagrand in 2014: TWIC's crosstable and all 435 PGN headers confirm 20.5/29, correcting its introductory prose's 29-point total.

Nickel's interim 2014 centaur score, 53.5/96, was colour-imbalanced, with 54 White games and 42 Black. Incomplete and changing live assistance modes, unequal hardware, and an unverified eighth-edition winner prevent these event histories from estimating a causal collaboration premium; engine-name suffixes in TWIC headers cannot supply those labels.
