# research plan

The learning corpus cannot support historical band gaps because too few early games pass verification; the pilot estimates variance and search stability only. The [outline](essay-outline.md) uses published learning evidence and the completed local comparisons, with source pages in the [claims ledger](claims.md); the original [hypotheses](hypotheses.md) retain their dated amendment.

## completed comparisons

- Nielsen: both positions, Stockfish 13–16, and the three existing budgets; official bench signatures match for releases 4/8/12/13/14/15/16. Fruit's 67 unpatched mirror files match Debian's original after newline normalisation.
- Freestyle: final-32 circumstances classified, Williams identified with Ibermax, and all 435 TWIC 2014 headers audited. Live entry modes remain incomplete.
- Participation: January listed counts at 2250+, active counts at 2000/2250/2400, and the 2008–09 identity bridge. Online game counts remain a separate series; award files are retained outside the figure.
- Revised advice: 500 positions, a fifty-position budget check, and paired comparison with the within-release noise baseline. No opening-turnover sub-analysis.
- Pawn patterns: frozen h/a-pawn detector, ten checks, and restricted-pool counts; no historical adoption estimate.

## capped learning work

Six Lumbra batches match the archived hashes, with CC BY-NC 4.0 attribution checked; the old indexer's exclusions were audited before a new eligibility ledger and rating-band sampler were used. The pilot contains 252 player-games and 219 usable birth-year matches, with insufficient coverage for the proposed age comparison.

Corpus expansion stopped after the pilot: no main sample size or search budget is frozen from these data, and no historical learning curve is fitted.

Bilalić OSF calibration waits for maintenance to end; rescore a presampled set only when the original games and code are accessible.

A resumed main study would first require verified classical events across the missing early strata and reconciliation of player ratings before a dated amendment could freeze sample sizes and confirmation budgets: retain moves 9–40 expected-score loss as primary, moves 11–60 accuracy and error rates as secondary; annotate the four historical milestones without searching for breakpoints. Within-player change and turnover require separate estimates where identities permit.

## deferred work

Maia mapping remains deferred. A novelty analysis would require a fixed-size reference sample because archive growth distorted the retired departure measure; search-speed scenarios remain labelled uncalibrated and excluded from the essay.
